💡 Our Solution: VoiceGuard

Multi-signal AI analysis that detects scams before they succeed

Core Innovation: Multi-Signal Analysis

Unlike single-factor solutions that rely only on voice or only on keywords, VoiceGuard combines 5 independent signals to detect sophisticated scams with 95%+ accuracy.

🎙️

1. Voice Acoustic Analysis

Detects AI-generated voices by analyzing pitch jitter, spectral artifacts, prosody patterns, and phase discontinuities that neural vocoders leave behind.

💬

2. Conversation Semantic Analysis

NLP-powered detection of scam patterns: OTP requests, urgency tactics, financial demands, threatening language, and social engineering keywords.

🔍

3. Caller Identity Verification

Cross-references caller with trusted contacts database, validates voice biometric profiles, and detects identity mismatches.

🌐

4. Metadata & Telecom Analysis

Identifies VoIP calls, spoofed caller IDs, international routing anomalies, and suspicious carrier patterns.

🧠

5. Behavioral Pattern Detection

Analyzes call timing, conversation flow, emotional pressure tactics, and deviation from typical communication patterns.

⚖️

Explainable AI

Transparent risk scoring with clear reasoning. Users see exactly WHY a call was flagged with weighted contribution breakdown.

✨ Unique Features

What makes VoiceGuard different

⚖️

Explainable AI (XAI)

Every risk score comes with transparent reasoning. Users see exactly which factors contributed what percentage to the final score. No black-box decisions.

35% Voice + 25% Conversation + 20% Financial + 12% Caller + 8% Metadata = 94/100 Risk
👥

Voice Biometric Profiles

Users enroll trusted contacts with voice samples. System generates 512-dimensional ECAPA-TDNN embeddings for accurate speaker verification.

📄

1930 Portal Integration

One-click forensic report generation for National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Includes audio evidence, transcript, and AI analysis results.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Alert System

Automatically notifies trusted emergency contacts when high-risk scam attempts are detected. Real-time SMS/WhatsApp alerts.

🌐

Multilingual Support

Voice analysis works across all Indian languages. Conversation analysis supports Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi using multilingual NLP models.

🔒

Privacy-First Design

End-to-end encryption, local voice profile storage, user-controlled data deletion, minimal retention policy. GDPR/DPDPA compliant architecture.
