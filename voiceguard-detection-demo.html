🎯 How VoiceGuard Detects AI Cloning

  • Prosody Analysis: AI voices have unnaturally smooth pitch transitions, while real voices have natural micro-variations
  • Pitch Jitter: Human voices naturally fluctuate (0.015-0.04), while AI clones are too stable (< 0.012)
  • Phase Coherence: AI-generated audio has suspiciously coherent phase patterns across frequencies
  • Spectral Artifacts: Voice cloning tools leave subtle frequency domain artifacts that real voices don't have
  • Temporal Consistency: AI lacks the organic micro-variations in timing that humans naturally produce
  • Harmonic Structure: AI voices are often "too perfect" with unnaturally clean harmonic relationships
