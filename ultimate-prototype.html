🔍 How VoiceGuard Works

VoiceGuard uses a 5-layer AI detection system combining Acoustic Analysis (spectral patterns), Prosodic Analysis (speech rhythm), CNN Classifier (deep learning), RNN Temporal (time-series), and Voice Biometrics (unique voice DNA). All threats are logged on blockchain for immutable evidence.
