🧪 Technical Details to Explain (For Judges' Questions)

How We Detect AI Voices:

Feature Real Voice AI Voice
Pitch Jitter 0.08-0.15 (natural variation) 0.01-0.02 (too stable)
Spectral Flux Irregular, organic patterns Repetitive, neural artifacts
Pause Duration 200-400ms between words 30-50ms (too fast)
Frequency Range Wide, natural harmonics Compressed, synthesized
Prosody Dynamic, emotional Flat, monotone

Our Detection Algorithm (Simplified):

1. Extract MFCC features (Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients) 2. Analyze pitch contour and jitter 3. Detect phase discontinuities in spectrogram 4. Compare against known human voice baselines 5. Calculate synthetic probability score (0-100%) 6. Combine with conversation + caller analysis 7. Output: Risk Score + Explainability
