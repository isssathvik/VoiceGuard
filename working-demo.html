💡 How It Works

VoiceGuard uses a 5-layer AI detection system: Acoustic Analysis (spectral patterns), Prosodic Analysis (speech rhythm), CNN Classifier (deep learning), RNN Temporal (time-series analysis), and Voice Biometrics (unique voice DNA). All detections are logged on blockchain for immutable evidence.

🔐 Why Blockchain?

Every detected threat is permanently recorded on blockchain, creating an immutable evidence trail. This ensures reports cannot be tampered with and provides legal validity for cybercrime investigations. The distributed ledger is shared across law enforcement, banks, and security agencies.
