📋 What Blockchain Does in VoiceGuard

1

AI Detects Fake Voice

The 5-layer AI detection system analyzes the call and determines it's an AI-cloned voice with 94% confidence

↓
2

Threat Data Packaged

System creates a tamper-proof record containing: caller details, detection confidence, timestamp, audio fingerprint (not the actual audio), and threat classification

↓
3

Blockchain Transaction Created

The threat record is hashed and submitted as a transaction to Ethereum blockchain (or private consortium chain for enterprises)

↓
4

Immutable Evidence Stored

Transaction is confirmed and permanently recorded. Law enforcement, banks, and cybercrime cells can verify the evidence was never altered

📊 What Gets Stored on Blockchain

🔐 Privacy-Preserving Data Structure

Stored on blockchain: Detection metadata (timestamp, confidence score, threat classification, audio fingerprint hash)

NOT stored: Actual audio recordings, caller names, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information

Why: Blockchain is immutable—only non-sensitive metadata goes on-chain. Audio stays in encrypted secure storage with proper access controls.
